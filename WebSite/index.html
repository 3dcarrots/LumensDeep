Core Pillars

Engineered for Extreme Fidelity

Designed from the ground up for high-dynamic-range camera sensors, computational photography researchers, and discerning portrait photographers.

🔇

AI Neural Denoising (NAFNet-SIDD In-Memory)

State-of-the-art Nonlinear Activation Free Network pre-trained on SIDD (40.30 dB PSNR). Denoises 32-bit linear sensor data directly in-memory before tone mapping, eliminating Poisson-Gaussian shot noise while preserving organic skin pores, eyelashes, and fabric micro-textures via spatial cosine-blended tiling.

🧠

Gemini 3.7 Flash AI & One-Time Prompts

Interactive multimodal co-pilot with deep Thinking reasoning. Features separated Studio Retouching Rules, instant One-Time Prompts (with auto-clear & quick chips), 50% dampened convergence, and AI Bokeh A/B verification.

🎯

2-Phase AI Culling & Resilient Batch Automation

Phase 1 rapid triage and Phase 2 Center 50% Crop optical blur inspection. Includes 1-Click Full Auto Batch grading with resilient 10s persistent retry, automated ISO > 200 denoise, and smart skip resume.

🚀

32-Bit Linear GPU & Hardware Anti-Aliasing

All calculations run on 32-bit floating-point buffers in ModernGL. Hardware trilinear mipmap pyramids eliminate sensel aliasing, moiré, and downsampling noise on 45MP Canon CR3 / Sony ARW files.

✨

3D Bokeh & Interactive Focus Picker

True optical depth-of-field simulation using Monte Carlo Vogel spiral raymarching. Includes one-click 3D Canvas Focus Eyedropper, cat-eye vignetting, chromatic aberration, and polygonal iris blades.

🎞️

Aspect Subfolder Sorting & Fast Viewer

Auto-slices exports into dedicated aspect subfolders (Original, Photo_iPad_4x5, Portrait_2x3, Square_1x1, Story_3x4, Cinematic_16x9) with 5★ master curation, plus sub-50ms instant Fast Viewer browsing.

📊

OKLCH Tone & Color Equalizers

Targeted 8-band luminance and hue adjustments in perceptual OKLCH color space. Brighten deep shadows or tone skies without unwanted saturation shifts.

🖌️

Sub-Pixel GPU Brush Masking

Hand-paint masks with hardware-accelerated stroke interpolation. Rapid mouse sweeps produce smooth, continuous lines with zero gaps or stepping.

⚡

NVIDIA CUDA & DirectML AI Speedup

Heterogeneous GPU acceleration for DepthAnythingV2. Batched FP16 TensorCore inference delivers ~12x faster depth maps (0.024s/tile) with DirectML and Safe Mode CPU fallback.

🔌

FastMCP Server for AI Agents

Built-in Model Context Protocol server (SSE & stdio on port 8000) allowing external AI coding assistants to inspect histograms, extract crops, and apply live adjustments.

Behind the Code

“Every RAW photo is a 3D scene waiting to be unlocked — not just a flat grid of pixels.”

Hi, I’m Volodymyr Makovetskyi. As the Lead Developer of 3DCoat and creator of Scanimat technology (scanimat.com, scanimate.io), my journey in 3D computer graphics began back in 1995, with over 20 years of commercial engineering experience spanning from early 3D model architectures to high-end real-time GPU rendering engines.

I originally began building Lumens Deep as a personal hobby project to edit family photos with the quality, optical realism, and depth fidelity I always dreamed of. Traditional industry editors like Adobe Lightroom, Capture One, and Darktable are fantastic for flat 2D color grading, but none of them generate ultra-precise 40MP+ metric depth maps where every single pixel aligns perfectly with RAW sensor resolution. None of them allow you to treat a capture as a living 3D environment — isolating depth planes, relighting surfaces via high-frequency surface normals, or computing genuine optical bokeh raymarching with customizable aperture blade geometry.

🤖 The Lumens Deep AI Philosophy: Authenticity Over Hallucination

Lumens Deep was purposefully engineered for the next era of AI Agent collaboration via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Unlike generative diffusion tools that hallucinate and redraw over your photos, AI agents in Lumens Deep act as precision studio assistants — they analyze and adjust the native GPU sliders, OKLCH curves, and 3D geometric masks without ever painting over or compromising your photograph’s genuine optical authenticity.

I am sharing Lumens Deep with the global photography and developer community as a 100% free and open-source project (MIT License). It is built to give visual artists total creative freedom, without subscriptions, walled gardens, or cloud dependencies.
