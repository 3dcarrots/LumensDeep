## 💡 Quick Tips for Taking Great Screenshots:
1. **Resolution:** Use a standard 1080p (`1920×1080`) or 1440p/4K display scaling so text and UI icons look crisp and sharp.
2. **Lighting / Photos:** Use a high-quality portrait or landscape photo with good dynamic range and depth for the best aesthetic presentation.
3. **Format:** Save in `.png` (lossless) or high-quality `.jpg` ($95\%$ quality).
